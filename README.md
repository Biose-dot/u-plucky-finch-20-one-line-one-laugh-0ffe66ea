One funny line of at most 200 characters. The house judge reads entries two at a time
and picks the funnier one.

> My Roomba wedged itself under the couch and now just lives there, judging me from the dark.

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only) copies `entry.txt` to `/output/entry.txt`:

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```
